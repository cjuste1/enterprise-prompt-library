## Purpose
Generate clear, compelling internal announcements 
for AI tool rollouts that reduce resistance and 
build excitement — written for non-technical audiences.

---

## Industries
All industries implementing AI tools

---

## Input Variables
| Variable | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| {{tool_name}} | Name of the AI tool being introduced |
| {{department}} | Team or department receiving the tool |
| {{key_benefit}} | Primary benefit for this specific team |
| {{start_date}} | Rollout date |
| {{contact_name}} | Point of contact for questions |
| {{company_voice}} | formal / warm / casual |

---

## The Prompt
```
You are an internal communications specialist 
with expertise in AI adoption and change management.

Your job is to write an announcement that makes 
employees excited — not afraid — about a new AI tool.

Tool Name: {{tool_name}}
Department: {{department}}
Key Benefit: {{key_benefit}}
Start Date: {{start_date}}
Contact: {{contact_name}}
Company Voice: {{company_voice}}

Write an internal announcement email that includes:

SUBJECT LINE: [compelling, benefit-focused]

OPENING: [1-2 sentences addressing the human side 
first — not the technology]

WHAT IS CHANGING: [2-3 sentences, simple language, 
no jargon]

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU: [3 bullet points — 
personal benefits, not company benefits]

YOUR FIRST STEP: [one simple action, 
this week, takes under 5 minutes]

QUESTIONS: [who to contact and how]

CLOSING: [warm, encouraging, forward-looking]
```

---

## Performance Targets
- Employee open rate tracking
- Resistance reduction in follow up survey
- First step completion rate >60%
- Manager satisfaction with tone >4/5

---

## Iteration Notes
- Always lead with human benefit not technology feature
- One call to action only — more than one reduces completion
- Avoid words: implement, leverage, utilize, deploy
- Use words: try, help, save, easier, faster, you
- Test subject lines — benefit-first outperforms feature-first

---

## Example Output
```
SUBJECT LINE: Your team just got a new colleague 
— and it never takes a lunch break

OPENING: We know change can feel overwhelming, 
especially when it involves new technology. 
That is why we wanted to tell you about this 
one before it arrives.

WHAT IS CHANGING: Starting Monday March 10th, 
your team will have access to Microsoft Copilot 
inside Outlook and Teams. It is an AI assistant 
that lives right where you already work — 
no new apps, no new logins.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU:
- Summarize long email threads in seconds
- Turn rough meeting notes into clean action items
- Draft professional emails from bullet points

YOUR FIRST STEP: Open Outlook on Monday and 
try this one prompt — Summarize this email 
thread and list my action items.
That is it. Two minutes. See what happens.

QUESTIONS: Reach out to Sarah Johnson on Teams 
or email enablement@company.com
```
